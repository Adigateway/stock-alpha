What this is

One Feature. That's the Whole Thing.

Stock Alpha ranks S&P 500 constituents by their six-month price return and surfaces the top fifteen. There is no machine learning model, no probability output, and no tuning. The number you see beside each ticker is its actual six-month return, not a prediction.

It got here by elimination. A gradient-boosted model on 38 features was built, tested, and removed — under blind validation it scored an AUC of 0.50 with near-zero rank correlation, no better than a coin flip. Fundamental screens were tested three separate ways and each one reduced returns. Momentum was the only thing that survived a clean test.

01
DATA
Daily prices from Yahoo Finance and quarterly filings from SEC EDGAR. Filings carry a 45-day lag so nothing is read before it was public.
02
RANK
Six-month return per ticker, computed from raw unwinsorised prices, ranked across the universe. Fundamentals are shown for screening but play no part in the ranking.
03
OUTPUT
Top fifteen flagged BUY, each labelled by profitability so money-losing names are visible rather than buried. Refreshed quarterly.
The record

Real, Modest, and Regime-Dependent

Five snapshots, 2021 through 2025. Training data stops six months before each one, because a stock's six-month outcome is not known until six months later. Top fifteen, held six months, equal weighted, versus the whole universe.

Two of five periods were negative, and 2025 carries most of the average — that was the AI and semiconductor rally. Excluding it the edge is roughly five percent. Momentum works in trending markets and struggles in choppy ones. None of these figures include transaction costs, and turnover runs near forty percent a month.

⚠ PLEASE READ

Stock Alpha is a personal research project provided for informational and educational purposes only. Nothing here is financial, investment, or trading advice.

Momentum ranks stocks by price, not by business quality. The list regularly includes companies that are losing money — they are labelled, and you should check them yourself. The screen also concentrates heavily in whichever sector is running, which is the opposite of diversification.

Historical results do not predict future performance, and this record covers a single market regime with no prolonged bear market in it. Never invest based solely on this tool.
